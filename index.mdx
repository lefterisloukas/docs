Easy Edgar is a REST API for SEC EDGAR financial data. It lets you look up any public company, browse their filings, and extract clean text from specific sections — like Risk Factors or MD&A — from 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K forms going back to 1993. No parsing HTML dumps, no inconsistent formatting, no fighting with the raw SEC archives.

## Get started in 3 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your API key">
    Sign up and retrieve your Bearer token from your account dashboard. Your key starts with `hv_live_`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look up a company">
    Pass a ticker or CIK to `GET /v1/companies/{identifier}` to retrieve the company's profile and SEC metadata.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extract filing items">
    Use `GET /v1/filings/{accession_number}/items/{item_key}` to pull a specific section — like `item_1a` for Risk Factors — from any supported filing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why Easy Edgar?

The SEC publishes raw filing data publicly, but working with it is painful. Filings are HTML and text dumps with inconsistent formatting — especially pre-2009 — and there's no endpoint that cleanly returns "give me Item 7 from Apple's 2005 10-K."

Easy Edgar solves that. Its key differentiator is **item-level text extraction going back to 1993**, including the messy historical filings that commercial APIs either skip or charge heavily to access.
